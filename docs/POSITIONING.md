## One-line pitch

Point Claude Code at any high-performing short video, get a Style Blueprint of its hook, shots, pacing, captions, audio and CTA, then generate a new video on your topic and brand.

## 5 tagline options

1. Steal the structure, not the footage.
2. Reverse-engineer a viral video, then make your own.
3. From reference reel to your reel in three slash commands.
4. Analyze. Mimic. Generate.
5. Your best-performing video, as a reusable blueprint.

## Target users

- Solo creators and faceless-channel operators making TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- Small-brand marketers and agencies producing many ad or UGC variants.
- Indie hackers and founders who need product videos without an editor.
- Developers already using Claude Code who want a scriptable video pipeline.
- Growth and creative-strategy teams who want to document why a video works.
